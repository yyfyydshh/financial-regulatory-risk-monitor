# Financial-regulatory applicability rules

Apply narrower rules before broader thematic relevance.

## Source and effect

- Prefer the official National Financial Regulatory Administration domain and official provincial or local bureau pages. Treat mirrors, reposts, and snippets as pending verification unless the official text is also available.
- Preserve the publishing authority exactly, then normalize its level: national, Guangdong provincial, or Guangdong local branch.
- Distinguish publication date from effective date. A notice can announce a future obligation.
- Drafts, consultation papers, interpretations, meeting notices, lists, and general publicity may indicate direction but do not automatically create a binding obligation.
- If a document repeals another instrument, determine the status of the instrument that supplies the proposed obligation; do not label the repealing notice itself “expired” merely because it contains “废止”.

## Applicability gates

- `jurisdiction_match`: the enterprise operates, is licensed, has customers, or carries out the relevant activity in the covered location. A web channel alone does not prove every local rule applies.
- `subject_match`: the document expressly covers the enterprise's institution category or a demonstrably broader class that contains it.
- `activity_match`: the enterprise actually performs the regulated product, channel, outsourcing, data, AML, consumer-protection, or governance activity.
- `time_match`: the relevant activity occurs within the document's effective period.

An indirect vendor, technology provider, or intermediary relationship may create contractual/control exposure without making the enterprise the direct regulatory addressee. Label that distinction.

## Penalty treatment

- Extract the sanctioned party, violation conduct, cited legal basis, decision authority, decision date, and result.
- Aggregate repeated conduct only after de-duplication. Count decisions, not copied web pages.
- Use patterns to suggest controls for review. Do not infer that the profiled enterprise committed the conduct.
- A large fine or license measure increases consequence relevance but does not, by itself, establish enterprise applicability or probability.

## Professional wording

Use “命中画像事实”“可能适用”“需核实”“监管执法关注点” and “建议控制复核”. Avoid “已违法”“必然处罚” or a definitive legal conclusion unless the customer supplies verified facts and qualified legal review confirms it.
